## Our Mission

The Florida Forward Initiative was established by the Executive Office of the Governor to coordinate and accelerate strategic investments across the state. By aligning resources from multiple agencies and leveraging partnerships with the private sector and local communities, the initiative aims to deliver transformative results in four key areas: infrastructure modernization, workforce readiness, economic diversification, and community resilience.

## Why Now

Florida's population has grown by over 2 million residents since 2020, making it the fastest-growing large state in the nation. This growth creates both opportunity and urgency. Roads, bridges, broadband networks, housing, and public services must keep pace with demand. At the same time, Florida has a unique window to attract high-value industries, train a next-generation workforce, and strengthen communities against climate and economic disruption.

## Leadership

The initiative is housed within the Executive Office of the Governor and operates through a coordinating council that includes representatives from the Department of Economic Opportunity, the Department of Transportation, the Department of Education, and Enterprise Florida. Day-to-day operations are led by a dedicated program office with direct reporting to the Governor's senior staff.

## Strategic Goals

**Infrastructure Modernization** — Accelerate critical transportation, broadband, and utility projects using data-driven prioritization and streamlined permitting processes.

**Workforce Readiness** — Expand training programs, apprenticeships, and credentialing pathways aligned to high-demand industries including technology, healthcare, advanced manufacturing, and logistics.

**Economic Diversification** — Attract and retain businesses in emerging sectors, support small business growth, and strengthen Florida's position as a destination for investment.

**Community Resilience** — Invest in hazard mitigation, housing stability, and essential services that help communities withstand and recover from disruption.
