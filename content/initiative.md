## Program Areas

The Florida Forward Initiative operates across four interconnected program areas, each with defined targets and accountability measures.

### Infrastructure Modernization

Coordinating over $14 billion in planned infrastructure investments across transportation, broadband, water, and energy systems. Key projects include interstate corridor improvements, rural broadband expansion, and resilient utility upgrades in coastal communities.

### Workforce Development

Partnering with Florida's state college system, CareerSource network, and private employers to create 50,000 new credentialed positions in high-demand fields by 2028. Programs emphasize earn-while-you-learn models, industry-recognized certifications, and direct employer partnerships.

### Economic Growth

Working with Enterprise Florida and local economic development organizations to attract $5 billion in new private investment over three years. Priority sectors include aerospace and defense, life sciences, financial technology, and advanced logistics.

### Community Resilience

Deploying $2.3 billion in federal and state funds for hazard mitigation, affordable housing, and essential services. Projects are prioritized using a data-driven vulnerability index that accounts for flood risk, housing cost burden, and access to healthcare and transportation.
